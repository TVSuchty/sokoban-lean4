# Verified Sokoban Deadlock Detector, Counterexamples & Solver in Lean 4

A standalone, zero-dependency Lean 4 formalization (`leanprover/lean4:v4.37.0-rc1`) of **Sokoban deadlock detectors**, **formal counterexamples to unsound solver heuristics**, and a **certifying deadlock-pruned BFS solver**.

> **Honest Disclaimer on Authorship & Expertise:**
> I am not a Lean 4 or formal-mathematics expert—the Lean 4 proofs, counterexamples, and documentation in this repository were generated by AI from my prompts, with me guiding the direction.
>
> **Verification Guarantee:** You do not have to trust me or the AI blindly: every single theorem is 100% mechanically checked by the Lean 4 kernel (`leanprover/lean4:v4.37.0-rc1`) with zero `sorry`s, zero `native_decide`, and only Lean's standard axioms `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`—and the core Sokoban rules in [`Sokoban/Defs.lean`](Sokoban/Defs.lean) are short and easy to audit yourself.

## Highlights

1. **Unified Reachability-Aware Freeze Theorem (`superFreezeDeadlock_sound`):**
   Combines backward-BFS **Simple Dead Squares** (`computeLiveSquares`), iterative **Freeze Deadlocks** (`computeSuperFrozen`), and **topological player reachability** (`CanWalkAvoiding`) into one greatest-fixed-point deadlock detector, proving `¬ Solvable L s` whenever any non-goal box remains frozen.
2. **Three Tempting Heuristics Formally Refuted (`Sokoban/Counterexamples.lean`):**
   * `naiveDeadSquareFreeze_unsound`: Treating Simple Dead Squares like walls on the *pushing* side falsely flags `##### / #@$.# / #####` (solvable in 1 move).
   * `naiveCurrentReachFreeze_unsound`: Checking player walkability avoiding *all* current boxes instead of only frozen ones falsely flags a 4-move solvable 6×6 board.
   * `naiveGoalLock_unsound`: Locking boxes already on goals (`*`) turns a 12-move solvable 7×4 level into an unsolvable one.
3. **Certifying Solver (`Sokoban/Solver.lean`):**
   `solveBFS` prunes deadlocks via `isSuperFreezeDeadlockWithLive`; solvable levels are verified via `verifySolution_sound`, and unsolvable levels emit a closed state-space certificate verified inside Lean's kernel via `checkUnsolvableCert_sound` (demonstrated on a puzzle where `isSuperFreezeDeadlock = false` at turn 0!).
