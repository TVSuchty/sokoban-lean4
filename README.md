# Verified Sokoban Deadlock Detectors, PI-Corrals & Counterexamples in Lean 4

A standalone, zero-dependency Lean 4 formalization (`leanprover/lean4:v4.37.0-rc1`) of **Sokoban deadlock detectors**, **PI-Corral & merged corral pruning (with push-optimality)**, **formal counterexamples to real-world pruning bugs**, and a **certifying search checker**.

> **Honest Disclaimer on Authorship & Expertise:**
> I am not a Lean 4 or formal-mathematics expert—the Lean 4 proofs, counterexamples, and documentation in this repository were generated by AI from my prompts, with me guiding the direction.
>
> **Verification Guarantee:** You do not have to trust me or the AI blindly: every single theorem is 100% mechanically checked by the Lean 4 kernel (`leanprover/lean4:v4.37.0-rc1`) with zero `sorry`s, zero `native_decide`, and only Lean's standard axioms `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`, and additionally reviewed in a read-only audit by `gpt-6.2-astra` (`codex-cli`, with all identified API/model/documentation findings subsequently addressed). The core Sokoban rules in [`Sokoban/Defs.lean`](Sokoban/Defs.lean) are short and easy to audit yourself.

## Highlights

1. **Verified PI-Corral & Merged Corral Push-Optimality (`Sokoban/PICorral.lean`):**
   * Formalizes Dead-Square-Aware **PI-Corral pruning** (`IsPICorral`) uniformly over both **single corrals** and **arbitrarily merged multi-room corrals**, plus a greedy `findPICorral` merger.
   * Proves **`piCorral_push_optimal`**: whenever `IsPICorral` holds on a solvable board, at least one optimal solution starts with an immediate inward push into `C` (after pure walking), reducing pushes strictly (`k = k' + 1`). Also yields `piCorral_deadlock_sound` and `isPICorralBool_sound`.
   * Formally nails down the **I-vs-P external-box asymmetry** noted in YASS/SokoBoy: external boxes `s₀.boxes \ B` **must** be stripped in the I-Corral outward-push test, and **must not** be stripped in the P-Corral player-reachability test.
2. **Real-World PI-Corral Implementation Bug Counterexamples (`Sokoban/PICorralCounterexamples.lean`):**
   * `exMergedPICorral_verified`: Shows a board where neither pocket is a PI-Corral alone, yet merging produces a valid 2-push-optimal `IsPICorral`.
   * `piCorral_notStripExtI_unsound`: Forgetting to strip external boxes in the I-check causes `naivePICorralNoStripI` to force a fatal push on a 2-push solvable board (`picex1Board`).
   * `piCorral_stripExtP_unsound`: Stripping external boxes in the P-check triggers a false dynamic deadlock on a 5-move solvable board (`picex2Board`).
   * `piCorral_noUnsolvedObligation_unsound`: Omitting the unsolved-box obligation (`∃ b ∈ B, L.isGoal b = false`) triggers a false dynamic deadlock on an already-solved corral (`picex3Board`, solvable in 3 moves).
3. **Unified Reachability-Aware Freeze Theorem (`superFreezeDeadlock_sound`):**
   Combines backward-BFS **Simple Dead Squares** (`computeLiveSquares`), iterative **Freeze Deadlocks** (`computeSuperFrozen`), and **topological player reachability** (`CanWalkAvoiding`) into one fixed-point deadlock detector, proving `¬ Solvable L s` whenever any non-goal box remains frozen.
4. **Three Tempting Deadlock Heuristics Refuted (`Sokoban/Counterexamples.lean`):**
   * `naiveDeadSquareFreeze_unsound`, `naiveCurrentReachFreeze_unsound`, and `naiveGoalLock_unsound` (refuting both permanent goal locking and forbidding goal-to-non-goal pushes `StepNoOffGoalPush` on an 8×4 board).
5. **Certifying Solver & Standalone Checker (`Sokoban/Solver.lean`):**
   `solveBFS` prunes deadlocks via `isSuperFreezeDeadlockWithLive` and emits checkable witnesses (`.solved moves` or `.unsolvable (.initialDeadlock | .closedRegion visited)`). `checkUnsolvableCert` validates all structural premises internally, and `verifySolveResult` unifies both outcomes.
